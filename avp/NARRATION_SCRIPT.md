## 6. The Complete ISKONNECT Process

### 3:47.1–3:57.3 · `journey_intro`

**Visuals:** Chapter 05. A path with 12 milestones; a student marker travels the whole journey. A 12-dot progress rail stays on screen for the steps.

- `3:50.1` Now, let's follow one student's journey through ISKONNECT, from application, to graduation, and beyond.

### 3:57.3–4:21.4 · `step1_app` — Step 1: Application

**Visuals:** Phone: splash → login → applicant profile → scholarship application → 8 requirements uploaded (Pending → Submitted) → Application Submitted; new row appears in admin Applications Management (authorized access only).

- `3:57.9` It starts when an applicant opens the ISKONNECT mobile app.
- `4:01.4` They register or log in, set up their applicant profile, and fill out the scholarship application.
- `4:07.6` Next, the requirements are uploaded digitally. No long lines, and no stacks of photocopies.
- `4:13.7` Once submitted, the applicant's information and documents become accessible to authorized CED administrators.

### 4:21.4–4:38.9 · `step2_sched` — Step 2: Examination & Interview

**Visuals:** Status stepper Pending → For Exam → For Interview; phone notifications and schedule cards; admin table + October calendar marking exam and interview.

- `4:22.0` As the application moves forward, the applicant receives the examination schedule right in the app.
- `4:28.1` After the examination, the interview schedule follows.
- `4:31.6` On the admin dashboard, CED manages these schedules and keeps every applicant's information organized.

### 4:38.9–5:06.7 · `step3_eval` — Step 3: Evaluation & Selection

**Visuals:** Examination + Economic Background + Panel Interview → ranking board (bars grow, rows re-order) → Selection slots based on annual budget → "Final selection by CED"; "ISKONNECT assists & records · CED decides".

- `4:39.5` Next comes evaluation.
- `4:41.3` CED considers the examination, the applicant's economic background, and the panel interview.
- `4:47.6` Applicants are then evaluated and ranked, and CED makes the final selection.
- `4:52.7` The number of available scholar slots depends on the program's annual budget.
- `4:57.4` ISKONNECT does not decide who becomes a scholar. It helps CED manage and record each step of the evaluation and selection process.

### 5:06.7–5:23.5 · `step4_scholar` — Step 4: Becoming a Scholar

**Visuals:** Celebration screen with confetti; APPLICANT → ACTIVE SCHOLAR; digital scholar profile card.

- `5:07.3` For successful applicants, this is a milestone.
- `5:10.3` Their status changes from applicant to active scholar.
- `5:13.6` Each scholar now has a digital profile, with personal information, school, program, scholarship details, status, and academic information.

### 5:23.5–5:53.9 · `step5_semester` — Step 5: Semester Process

**Visuals:** Circular 8-step semester cycle with a travelling dot; subjects card; monitoring chips; Certificate of Grades upload ("scholars cannot encode their own grades"); admin Encode Grade panel; loops to 2nd Semester.

- `5:24.1` Every semester follows a clear, repeating cycle.
- `5:27.4` When the semester begins, the scholar submits their enrolled subjects.
- `5:31.5` Academic progress and attendance are monitored throughout the term, and requirements are submitted or updated as needed.
- `5:38.6` Scholars upload their Certificate of Grades, but they do not encode their own grades.
- `5:43.3` Official grades are encoded by authorized CED administrators.
- `5:47.7` CED then monitors the scholarship standing, and the cycle continues into the next semester.

### 5:53.9–6:21.8 · `step6_qr` — Step 6: QR Attendance

**Visuals:** Scholarship ID with QR → scanner phone reads it (beep) → Attendance recorded → offline "saved locally / pending sync" → synced → Admin Dashboard (pipeline row).

- `5:54.5` Attendance at scholarship activities is recorded using QR codes.
- `5:59.0` Each scholar has a personal QR code on their digital Scholarship ID.
- `6:03.6` At the event, CED staff scan it using the separate ISKONNECT QR Scanner app.
- `6:09.2` Each scan becomes an attendance record.
- `6:11.6` Without internet, records are stored locally on the device, then synchronized once the connection returns.
- `6:18.3` The attendance then appears on the admin dashboard.

### 6:21.8–6:42.5 · `step7_alert` — Step 7: Attendance Alert

**Visuals:** Attendance record reaches 2 absences → "At-Risk Scholar" alert on the admin dashboard → Attendance Alert → Admin Review → Administrative Decision; "No automatic termination".

- `6:22.4` ISKONNECT also helps CED keep an eye on attendance.
- `6:26.0` When a scholar reaches two absences, the system flags it and alerts the administrators.
- `6:31.4` The system does not terminate a scholarship on its own.
- `6:34.6` Instead, an administrator reviews the case and decides on the appropriate action, based on the scholar's circumstances.

### 6:42.5–7:04.7 · `step8_req` — Step 8: Requirements & Documents

**Visuals:** Scholar requirements list; Pending → Submitted → Verified pipeline; admin Requirements Review with Verify/Reject and typed remarks; Rejected → resubmit loop.

- `6:43.1` Requirements are easy to keep track of, too.
- `6:45.7` Scholars can see exactly which documents are needed, and upload them from the app.
- `6:50.7` Each requirement moves from Pending, to Submitted, to Verified once an administrator reviews it.
- `6:56.9` If a document needs correction, the administrator can return it with remarks, so the scholar knows exactly what to fix.

### 7:04.7–7:23.7 · `step9_comm` — Step 9: Announcements & Communication

**Visuals:** Admin types and posts an announcement → lock-screen notification → Announcements list (Announcement, Schedule, Reminder, Scholarship Update) → chat with CED.

- `7:05.3` Staying connected is at the heart of ISKONNECT.
- `7:08.3` An administrator creates an announcement on the dashboard,
- `7:11.7` and it reaches the student app, with a notification even when the app is closed.
- `7:16.2` Schedules, reminders, and scholarship updates arrive directly, and scholars can message CED through the app.

### 7:23.7–7:46.6 · `step10_reports` — Step 10: Reports & Monitoring

**Visuals:** Admin dashboard (illustrative data): KPI counters, scholars per school, status donut; organized-by chips; a "Total Active Scholars Report" with PDF/Excel export.

- `7:24.3` For CED, all of this information comes together on the admin dashboard.
- `7:29.1` Scholar and applicant records, academic information, attendance, requirements, and scholarship status are organized by school and category.
- `7:38.2` With charts, statistics, and ready-to-generate reports, CED can monitor scholars and prepare reports more efficiently.

### 7:46.6–8:13.8 · `step11_history` — Step 11: Scholarship Status & History

**Visuals:** Applicant → Scholar → Active → Graduated → Post-Graduation Tracking (Employed / Board Passer / Post-Graduate Studies); Stopped/Terminated branch recorded; 8 semester blocks join into one continuous record.

- `7:47.2` ISKONNECT keeps each scholar's history, from start to finish.
- `7:50.9` From applicant, to scholar, to active status.
- `7:53.8` If a scholarship is stopped or terminated, that decision is recorded as part of the history.
- `7:59.5` For graduates, tracking continues, whether they become employed, pass a board examination, or pursue post-graduate studies.
- `8:07.3` Instead of disconnected semester records, CED sees one continuous story for every scholar.

### 8:13.8–8:36.2 · `step12_cycle` — Step 12: The Complete Cycle

**Visuals:** Eleven stages light up in a serpentine path, then collapse into CED ↔ ISKONNECT ↔ SCHOLARS.

- `8:14.4` Put together, this is the complete ISKONNECT cycle.
- `8:17.6` Application, evaluation, selection, and becoming a scholar.
- `8:21.5` Semester monitoring, attendance, requirements, and grades.
- `8:25.3` Reports and monitoring, all the way to graduation and post-graduation tracking.
- `8:30.6` One continuous flow, connecting CED, ISKONNECT, and its scholars.
