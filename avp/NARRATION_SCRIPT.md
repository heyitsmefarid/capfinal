## 7. The Complete ISKONNECT Process

### 4:08.5–4:20.9 · `journey_intro`

**Visuals:** Chapter 05. Juan (the mascot) walks a path through all 12 milestones; a 12-dot progress rail stays up for the steps.

- `4:11.5` Now, let's follow Juan, a future scholar from Calapan, on his journey through ISKONNECT, from application, to graduation, and beyond.

### 4:20.9–4:45.0 · `step1_app` — Step 1: Application

**Visuals:** Phone: splash → login → applicant profile → scholarship application → 8 requirements uploaded → Application Submitted; new row in admin Applications Management.

- `4:21.5` It starts when an applicant opens the ISKONNECT mobile app.
- `4:25.0` They register or log in, set up their applicant profile, and fill out the scholarship application.
- `4:31.1` Next, the requirements are uploaded digitally. No long lines, and no stacks of photocopies.
- `4:37.3` Once submitted, the applicant's information and documents become accessible to authorized CED administrators.

### 4:45.0–5:02.5 · `step2_sched` — Step 2: Examination & Interview

**Visuals:** Status stepper Pending → For Exam → For Interview; phone notifications and schedule cards; admin table + October calendar.

- `4:45.6` As the application moves forward, the applicant receives the examination schedule right in the app.
- `4:51.7` After the examination, the interview schedule follows.
- `4:55.2` On the admin dashboard, CED manages these schedules and keeps every applicant's information organized.

### 5:02.5–5:30.2 · `step3_eval` — Step 3: Evaluation & Selection

**Visuals:** Examination + Economic Background + Panel Interview → ranking board → Selection slots (annual budget) → "Final selection by CED"; "ISKONNECT assists & records · CED decides".

- `5:03.1` Next comes evaluation.
- `5:04.9` CED considers the examination, the applicant's economic background, and the panel interview.
- `5:11.1` Applicants are then evaluated and ranked, and CED makes the final selection.
- `5:16.3` The number of available scholar slots depends on the program's annual budget.
- `5:21.0` ISKONNECT does not decide who becomes a scholar. It helps CED manage and record each step of the evaluation and selection process.

### 5:30.2–5:47.1 · `step4_scholar` — Step 4: Becoming a Scholar

**Visuals:** Celebration screen with confetti and Juan cheering; APPLICANT → ACTIVE SCHOLAR; digital scholar profile card.

- `5:30.8` For successful applicants, this is a milestone.
- `5:33.9` Their status changes from applicant to active scholar.
- `5:37.2` Each scholar now has a digital profile, with personal information, school, program, scholarship details, status, and academic information.

### 5:47.1–6:17.5 · `step5_semester` — Step 5: Semester Process

**Visuals:** Circular semester cycle with a travelling dot; subjects; monitoring; Certificate of Grades upload ("scholars cannot encode their own grades"); admin Encode Grade; loops to 2nd Semester.

- `5:47.7` Every semester follows a clear, repeating cycle.
- `5:50.9` When the semester begins, the scholar submits their enrolled subjects.
- `5:55.1` Academic progress and attendance are monitored throughout the term, and requirements are submitted or updated as needed.
- `6:02.1` Scholars upload their Certificate of Grades, but they do not encode their own grades.
- `6:06.9` Official grades are encoded by authorized CED administrators.
- `6:11.3` CED then monitors the scholarship standing, and the cycle continues into the next semester.

### 6:17.5–6:45.4 · `step6_qr` — Step 6: QR Attendance

**Visuals:** Scholarship ID QR → scanner (beep) → attendance recorded → offline, pending sync → synced → Admin Dashboard.

- `6:18.1` Attendance at scholarship activities is recorded using QR codes.
- `6:22.6` Each scholar has a personal QR code on their digital Scholarship ID.
- `6:27.2` At the event, CED staff scan it using the separate ISKONNECT QR Scanner app.
- `6:32.8` Each scan becomes an attendance record.
- `6:35.2` Without internet, records are stored locally on the device, then synchronized once the connection returns.
- `6:41.9` The attendance then appears on the admin dashboard.

### 6:45.4–7:06.1 · `step7_alert` — Step 7: Attendance Alert

**Visuals:** Record reaches 2 absences → "At-Risk Scholar" alert → Attendance Alert → Admin Review → Administrative Decision; "No automatic termination".

- `6:46.0` ISKONNECT also helps CED keep an eye on attendance.
- `6:49.6` When a scholar reaches two absences, the system flags it and alerts the administrators.
- `6:55.0` The system does not terminate a scholarship on its own.
- `6:58.2` Instead, an administrator reviews the case and decides on the appropriate action, based on the scholar's circumstances.

### 7:06.1–7:28.3 · `step8_req` — Step 8: Requirements & Documents

**Visuals:** Pending → Submitted → Verified pipeline; admin Requirements Review with Verify/Reject and remarks; Rejected → resubmit loop.

- `7:06.7` Requirements are easy to keep track of, too.
- `7:09.3` Scholars can see exactly which documents are needed, and upload them from the app.
- `7:14.2` Each requirement moves from Pending, to Submitted, to Verified once an administrator reviews it.
- `7:20.5` If a document needs correction, the administrator can return it with remarks, so the scholar knows exactly what to fix.

### 7:28.3–7:47.3 · `step9_comm` — Step 9: Announcements & Communication

**Visuals:** Admin posts an announcement → lock-screen notification → Announcements list (4 types) → chat with CED.

- `7:28.9` Staying connected is at the heart of ISKONNECT.
- `7:31.9` An administrator creates an announcement on the dashboard,
- `7:35.3` and it reaches the student app, with a notification even when the app is closed.
- `7:39.8` Schedules, reminders, and scholarship updates arrive directly, and scholars can message CED through the app.

### 7:47.3–8:10.1 · `step10_reports` — Step 10: Reports & Monitoring

**Visuals:** Admin dashboard (illustrative data): KPIs, scholars per school, status donut; "Total Active Scholars Report" with PDF/Excel export.

- `7:47.9` For CED, all of this information comes together on the admin dashboard.
- `7:52.7` Scholar and applicant records, academic information, attendance, requirements, and scholarship status are organized by school and category.
- `8:01.8` With charts, statistics, and ready-to-generate reports, CED can monitor scholars and prepare reports more efficiently.

### 8:10.1–8:37.4 · `step11_history` — Step 11: Scholarship Status & History

**Visuals:** Applicant → Scholar → Active → Graduated → Post-Graduation Tracking (Employed / Board Passer / Post-Graduate Studies); Stopped/Terminated branch; semesters join into one record.

- `8:10.7` ISKONNECT keeps each scholar's history, from start to finish.
- `8:14.4` From applicant, to scholar, to active status.
- `8:17.4` If a scholarship is stopped or terminated, that decision is recorded as part of the history.
- `8:23.1` For graduates, tracking continues, whether they become employed, pass a board examination, or pursue post-graduate studies.
- `8:30.9` Instead of disconnected semester records, CED sees one continuous story for every scholar.

### 8:37.4–8:59.7 · `step12_cycle` — Step 12: The Complete Cycle

**Visuals:** Eleven stages light up along a serpentine path, then collapse into CED ↔ ISKONNECT ↔ SCHOLARS.

- `8:38.0` Put together, this is the complete ISKONNECT cycle.
- `8:41.2` Application, evaluation, selection, and becoming a scholar.
- `8:45.1` Semester monitoring, attendance, requirements, and grades.
- `8:48.9` Reports and monitoring, all the way to graduation and post-graduation tracking.
- `8:54.1` One continuous flow, connecting CED, ISKONNECT, and its scholars.
